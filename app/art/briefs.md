# Drawing briefs

What each joker's drawing shows, for whoever draws it. A brief is approved
by the maintainer before the drawing is made, as names and quips are.

Style line, at the start of every prompt:

> pixel art game card illustration, front view, flat 2D, single subject
> centered, whole subject visible with a small margin, clean dark outline,
> few colors, transparent background, cute and funny, no text

A brief says what the drawing shows and gives the prompt that follows the
style line. The generator may wander from it: what matters is that the joke
reads at card size. Retro Diffusion draws single concrete objects and
characters well, abstract compositions (arrows, collisions) and fine detail
(keyboards) badly: prefer a pun that fits in one object.

Avoid the word "little" in prompts: Retro Diffusion then draws a tiny
subject in an empty frame. Keep subjects light-coloured too: it tends to
fill bodies with its dark purple, which reads as a dark blob.
