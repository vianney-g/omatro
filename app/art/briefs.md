# Drawing briefs

What each joker's drawing shows, for whoever draws it. A brief is approved
by the maintainer before the drawing is made, as names and quips are.

Style line, at the start of every prompt:

> pixel art game card illustration, single subject centered, clean outline,
> flat colors with simple shading, transparent background, cute and funny,
> no text

## Trial

### Uptime — *847 days without a reboot.*

+1 mult per hand in a row without a scoring face card.

- **Shows:** an old beige-ish PC tower that has never been rebooted. Cobwebs
  in a corner, a little spider hanging from the web on its thread, dust on
  top, a sticky note on the front scribbled in red (the "do not reboot"
  note, scribbles only), the power light still on.
- **Colours:** tower in muted / foreground, note in warm, scribbles in
  urgent, LED green.
- **Prompt:** an old dusty computer tower covered in cobwebs, a small spider
  hanging from a web, a sticky note on the front, green power light on

### Fork — *Same features. MIT licensed.*

Copies the ability of the joker on its right.

- **Shows:** a hand gripping a fork upright, eager to dig in. Keep it simple:
  the hand and the fork, nothing else. Outline and fork in foreground, hand
  orange, sleeve warm.
- **Prompt:** a cartoon hand gripping a fork upright, eager and hungry,
  shirt cuff at the wrist

### Rubber Duck — *Explains your bug back to you.*

+50 chips if the hand contains a Pair.

- **Shows:** a yellow rubber duck, smug and wise, with a small speech bubble
  holding a "!" (it found the bug). Duck warm with orange beak, bubble
  foreground.
- **Prompt:** a smug yellow rubber duck with a small speech bubble with an
  exclamation mark

### Low Battery — *Still at 2%. Still going.*

Adds twice the rank of the lowest card held in hand to mult.

- **Shows:** a battery with tiny legs, jogging on, exhausted but stubborn:
  a sweat drop, a thin red sliver of charge left. Battery foreground and
  muted, charge urgent, sweat cyan.
- **Prompt:** a tired cartoon battery with tiny legs running, almost empty
  with a thin red charge level, sweat drop, determined

### Segfault — *Undefined behavior, defined fun.*

+0 to +23 mult, at random.

- **Shows:** a small terminal window cracked in two, its halves out of line,
  glitched pixels spilling out of the crack. No text in the window. Window
  muted and foreground, glitch in magenta, cyan and urgent.
- **Prompt:** a small computer terminal window cracked in half, glitch
  pixels spilling out of the crack, digital error
